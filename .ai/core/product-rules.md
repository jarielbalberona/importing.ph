# Product Rules

Replace this file with project-specific product constraints.

- Do not invent business rules to fill gaps. Hard-stop for required product decisions.
- Treat authentication, privacy, billing, safety, moderation, destructive data changes, and trust workflows conservatively.
- Avoid dark patterns.
- Record durable product decisions in `.ai/state/decisions.md`.

If the project has no product surface, use this file for operational rules and owner expectations.
